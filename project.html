About

Esteban Bellantonio

UX/UI Designer & Frontend Developer

I'm a designer and developer based in Buenos Aires, focused on creating digital products that are both intuitive and visually refined. My work lives at the intersection of design thinking and engineering — I care as much about how something works as how it looks.

Over the past years I've worked extensively with Volkswagen Financial Services, designing and building platforms for credit onboarding, backoffice operations, and corporate client management. I work across the full product lifecycle: research, wireframing, prototyping in Figma, and frontend implementation in Angular/Ionic.

Currently at Dicsys, where I help teams build better digital experiences.
